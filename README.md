# Aroma & Crumb Artisan Café & Biscuit Bakery ☕🍪

A full-featured, responsive, production-ready web application for **Aroma & Crumb Artisan Café & Biscuit Bakery** — specializing in handcrafted hot and cold coffees, artisanal dipping biscuits, express thermal-packed home delivery, and instant WhatsApp ordering and table reservations.

🌐 **Live Website (GitHub Pages):** [https://iamanaypatel.github.io/cafe-website/](https://iamanaypatel.github.io/cafe-website/)

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. ☕ Comprehensive Coffee & Biscuit Menu (5 Categories + Pairing Engine)
- **Hot Coffees**: Classic Artisan Cappuccino, Madras Degree South Indian Filter Kaapi, Roasted Hazelnut Café Latte, Belgian Dark Chocolate Mocha, Spiced Jaggery (Gud) & Cardamom Latte, Double Shot Italian Espresso / Americano.
- **Cold Coffees & Frappes**: Iced Caramel Macchiato with Whipped Cream, Classic Café Thick Cold Coffee with Vanilla Ice Cream, 18-Hour Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew, Belgian Choco-Brownie Crunch Frappe.
- **Biscuits & Cookies (Coffee Dippers)**: Hyderabadi Osmania Royal Butter Biscuits (6 pcs), Warm Gooey Dark Choco-Chunk Cookies (4 pcs), Italian Twice-Baked Almond & Pistachio Biscotti (5 pcs), Desi Ghee Cardamom Nankhatai Shortbread (6 pcs), Crispy Layered Jeera & Ajwain Puff Khari (8 pcs), and The Royal Coffee-Dipper Assorted Tin Box (12 pcs).
- **Bakery Snacks**: Flaky French Butter Croissant with Chocolate Dip, Warm Walnut Chocolate Fudge Brownie.
- **Features**: Live search bar, category filtering pills, sort dropdown (Popularity, Price Low to High, Price High to Low), rating badges, dietary tags, customizable cup/pack size options, and a **"Best Paired With"** chip with a 1-click `+ Add Pair` button.

### 2. 🤖 AI Mood & Weather Combo Recommender
- Interactive mood pills:
  - ⚡ **Work & Focus Energy**: Double Shot Dark Roast + 2x Dark Choco-Chunk Cookies
  - 🌧️ **Cozy Rainy Day**: Spiced Jaggery & Cardamom Latte + 5x Almond Biscotti
  - ❄️ **Chill & Refreshing**: Iced Caramel Macchiato + 6x Osmania Butter Biscuits
  - 🇮🇳 **Classic Filter Kaapi**: Madras Degree Kaapi + 6x Desi Ghee Cardamom Nankhatai
- 10% combo discount automatically calculated with a 1-click `+ Add Pairing to Cart` button.

### 3. ✨ Custom Coffee & Biscuit Builder ("Custom Coffee Lab")
- **Live Visual SVG Cup & Biscuit Plate Preview**:
  - Interactive SVG illustration dynamically rendering liquid shade, milk foam opacity, whipped cream peaks, drizzle lines, steam animation for hot brews, and ice cubes for cold drinks.
  - Saucer with side dipping biscuits (Osmania, Choco-Chunk, or Almond Biscotti).
- **Step-by-Step Customization Controls**:
  - Cup size & temperature (Regular 200ml, Medium 300ml, Large Mug 400ml, Iced Glass 350ml)
  - Roast & extraction (Single Espresso, Double Dark Roast, South Indian Filter Decoction, 18-Hr Cold Brew)
  - Milk choice (Full Cream, Skimmed, Roasted Almond, Creamy Oat, Black)
  - Sweeteners (Cane Sugar, Organic Jaggery Gud, Demerara Brown, Zero-Cal Stevia, Unsweetened)
  - Multi-select toppings (Whipped Cream, Hazelnut Syrup, Caramel Drizzle, Belgian Cocoa Dust, Ceylon Cardamom)
  - Side Dipping Biscuit (3x Osmania, 2x Choco-Chunk, 2x Biscotti, or Coffee Only)
- Real-time Price, Estimated Calories (kcal), and Caffeine (mg) counter.

### 4. 🛵 Slide-Over Cart & Express Home Delivery
- **Order Mode Toggle**: 🛵 Home Delivery, 🛍️ Self Pickup, 🍽️ Café Dine-In.
- **Free Delivery Progress Meter**: Automatically unlocks free express shipping on orders over ₹299 with a live progress bar.
- **Distance-Based Delivery Estimator**: 0–3 km (20–25 mins), 3–6 km (30–35 mins), 6–10 km (40–45 mins).
- **Heat-Lock Packaging**: Toggleable 🔥 78°C Thermal Flask packaging (+₹25).
- **Working Promo Codes**: `COFFEE20` (20% off) & `BISCUIT40` (Flat ₹40 off).
- **Quick Upsell Strip**: 1-click cart add-ons for dipping biscuits.

### 5. 💬 Instant WhatsApp Ordering & Table Reservations
- **WhatsApp Checkout Dispatcher**: Formats a clean, itemized invoice with bold Markdown styling, timestamp, delivery address, order ID, and payment method (UPI on Delivery, COD, WhatsApp UPI).
- **WhatsApp Message Preview Modal**: Pre-formatted invoice review with a 1-click `📋 Copy Message` button and direct `🚀 Open WhatsApp & Send` link (`https://wa.me/919876543210?text=...`).
- **Interactive Café Seating Zone Reservations**: Select Cozy Work Pods (5G Wi-Fi & plugs), Garden Patio, Romantic Date Booths, or Family Sofas, with date/time pickers and pre-order Welcome Biscuit Platters (+₹149).

### 6. 🛵 4-Stage Live Delivery Tracker & Aroma Club Loyalty
- **Live 4-Stage Progress Tracker**:
  1. Order Confirmed & Payment Verified
  2. Barista Brewing & Packing Fresh Biscuits in Heat-Lock Box
  3. Rider Out for Delivery in Thermal Bag (78°C Sealed)
  4. Delivered at Doorstep
- Interactive simulation button to test delivery transitions.
- Direct WhatsApp courier support button.
- **Aroma Club Loyalty Wallet**: Earn 10 Bean Points per ₹100 spent. 1-click redemptions for free 4pc Osmania biscuits (80 pts), free espresso shot (100 pts), or free 2pc choco-chunk cookies (120 pts) at ₹0.

### 7. 🌐 Bilingual Interface (English & Hindi)
- Toggle between **English** and **हिंदी** with a single click in the top bar.
